Introduction

"Sakutto PDF Editor" (the "App") is a Windows app provided by Mojosoft Co., Ltd. ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Personal information

The App does not collect personal information.

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal information are collected.
  • No usage analytics or access logs are collected.
  • The App shows no ads and uses no cookies or tracking technologies.
  • No account is needed to use the App.

Processing of PDFs and images

Everything the App does — retyping, adding, moving, deleting and restyling text; merging, splitting, watermarking, extracting text, setting passwords, converting to and from images — happens only on your PC.

  • The PDFs and images you open, their contents and their file names are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Results are saved only to the location you choose.
  • When saving, the App first writes a temporary file in the destination folder and then renames it, so that an interrupted save cannot damage the original file.
  • The undo history is kept in memory only and is gone when you close the App.

Settings stored on your PC

The App stores only the following settings, in your Windows user profile on your PC. It does not store document contents or file names.

  • The window position and size
  • The default font name and size for added text, as chosen under "Settings…"
  • Whether the Microsoft Store review request has been shown, and the count used to decide when to show it

Internet use

The App does not use the internet to process PDFs or images. All features work without an internet connection.

  • While you use the App, it may ask you to leave a review on the Microsoft Store (if you choose "Cancel", it asks again after some more use; after "Yes" or "No" it does not ask again). Only if you choose "Yes" does it open the review page in the Microsoft Store.
  • Purchase, installation and updates of the App are handled by the Microsoft Store; the Microsoft Privacy Statement applies to the information involved.

Sharing with third parties

We do not collect information through the App, so we share none with third parties.

Children's privacy

The App collects no personal information, so it is safe for everyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. Changes will be posted on this page with the date of the update.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@mojosoft.biz
  • GitHub: Issues
